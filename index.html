CUNY AI Lab

Stefano Morello · Zach Muhlbauer · Steve Zweibel

CUNY AI Lab

AI Infrastructure for CUNY, by CUNY. A faculty- and staff-led initiative that develops and maintains AI tools for teaching, learning, and research — prioritizing privacy, transparency, and thoughtful integration of AI into academic work across the CUNY community.

Privacy by Design Built on providers that do not retain user data.
Transparency Favoring open-weight models.
Environmental Accountability Visibility into the costs of AI use.
Criticality AI literacy over AI dependency.
